# docs/review/

## Purpose
Pre-pitch reviews of the Hintwise MVP pitch, demo and demo content by three simulated panel members, plus a summary of
what we changed in response and what remains as talking points.

## Key decisions
- The demo was changed to show a **contrast** (Priya, the strong AI user, vs Jordan, the over-reliant one) and to run in the buyer's order: candidate → review → builder.
- **Integrity flags count only attempts to trick the AI**; asking for the answer is recorded as a behaviour, not an integrity issue.
- Business-model and market figures from the reviews are **not** adopted as facts; they remain talking points until the team agrees on them and their sources.
